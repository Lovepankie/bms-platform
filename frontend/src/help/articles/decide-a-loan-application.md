An application is appraised, then decided. You see only the step your role allows.

1. Open **Loans** and pick **Submitted** or **Appraised** to see the applications waiting.
   ![Applications waiting for a decision](decide-a-loan-application-1.webp)
2. Open the application. The button at the bottom takes it on: **Appraise** after the visit, then the decision.
   ![The next step at the bottom](decide-a-loan-application-2.webp)
3. To decide, choose **Approve** or **Decline**. For Approve, type the amount to lend and for how long, then tap **Approve the loan**. An approved loan is ready to pay out.
   ![Your decision](decide-a-loan-application-3.webp)

> If you sent or appraised the application, someone else must approve it. You can still decline it or send it back.
