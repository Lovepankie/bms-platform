A store keeps stock and does not sell. Sales are recorded only at a shop, so the sale screen asks you to choose one.

1. Look at the branch at the top of the menu. If it is a store, the sale screen says so and shows a **Sell at** button for each shop you work in.
   ![The sale screen at a store](why-no-sale-at-a-store-1.webp)
2. Tap **Sell at** and the shop's name, or use **Switch branch** at the top of the menu to choose the shop. Then record the sale as usual.
   ![Choose the shop to sell at](why-no-sale-at-a-store-2.webp)

> If the branch should sell, an administrator opens **Business set-up**, then **Branches**, and taps **Sells again (make it a shop)** on its card.
