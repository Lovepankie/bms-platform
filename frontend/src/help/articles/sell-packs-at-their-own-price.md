An item can come in packs, like a carton of 24 or a dozen. Each pack you sell can have its own price, so a carton does not have to cost 24 times a piece. Stock and profit are still counted per piece.

1. Tap **Edit** on the item. Under **Cartons, boxes and other packs**, choose the unit in **Add a pack** and type how many pieces it holds in **Items in one pack**.
2. Under **Used for**, choose **Selling** for a pack you only sell, like a half carton, **Buying** for one you only buy, or **Both**.
3. Type the **Selling price of one pack**. The app suggests the piece price times the size; leave it empty to use that. Then tap **Add pack**.
4. The table shows each pack with its size and price. To change one, tap **Change** on its row, then **Save**.
5. When you sell, choose the pack in **Sell by**. The sale takes the price of that pack, and so does scanning the barcode on the pack.

> A customer on a price list still gets the price of the list. **Below cost** next to a pack means its price is not above what its pieces cost you on average, so a sale at it needs permission to sell below cost.
