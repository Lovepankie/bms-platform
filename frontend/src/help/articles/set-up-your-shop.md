A new shop does not need to make its categories, units and packs one by one. Choose your kind of shop and the app lists what such a shop usually has.

1. On **Home** or **Sell**, tap **Set up your shop**. You can also open **Items and categories** and tap **Set up your shop** there.
2. Under **What kind of shop is it?**, tap the one closest to yours, such as **Hardware and building**. **Something else** gives just the common units.
3. Untick what you do not sell. For a pack, check its usual size, such as 100 Metre in one Roll, and choose under **Used for**: **Both**, **Selling** or **Buying**.
4. Tap **Add these**. The app makes the code letters of each new category. Names your shop has already are left as they are, so tapping it again adds nothing new.

> The usual size is only a starting number, and no price is set here. Open **Items** to add your items and set the price of each pack on the item.
