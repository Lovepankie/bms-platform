At the end of the day, check the cash, set aside the day's savings and record the cash you bank.

1. Open **Cash book** under Money. The card for each shop and day walks from the opening cash to the closing cash.
   ![The day's card in the cash book](close-the-day-1.webp)
2. Open **Savings**, pick the date and check the amount to set aside for the day. Tap **Save savings**.
   ![Set money aside for the day](close-the-day-2.webp)
3. Open **Banking**, look at the cash expected, type the amount you took to the bank and tap **Save banking**.
   ![Record the cash you banked](close-the-day-3.webp)

> Only people given "Can close the till and do the banking" see these pages. Ask your administrator if you need it.
