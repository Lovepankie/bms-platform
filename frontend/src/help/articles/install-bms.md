BMS works in your phone's browser. Installed, it opens like any other app from its icon, starts faster and can tell you when something needs you.

1. On the sign-in page, the card **Install the app** shows when this phone can install BMS. Tap **Install** and confirm.
2. Or, once signed in, open **Notifications** in the menu. Under **Install the app**, tap **Install the app** and confirm.
3. No Install button on an Android phone or a computer? Open the browser's menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**.
4. On an iPhone or iPad, use **Safari** in a normal tab, not a Private one. Tap Install as above and BMS shows the taps: **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
5. Open BMS from its new icon on your home screen.

> If you do not see Share, tap the address bar once so the buttons show. On an iPad, Share is at the top right.

> **Not now** hides the card on this phone. The Notifications page still has it.
