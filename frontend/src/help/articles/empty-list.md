title: What do I do when a list is empty?

When a list a form needs has nothing in it yet, the form says so in one line and shows the way to add what is missing, so you never have to look for it.

1. Look where the list should be. It says what is missing, such as **No units yet.**, with a button under it.
2. For a unit, a category, a supplier or a customer group, the button adds it right there. Tap it, such as + Add a unit, type the name and tap **Add unit**. It is added and chosen for you.
3. For a customer, a price list, a branch, an expense category, a product or a member, the button opens the screen that adds it, such as **Add a price list**. Once you add it there, the app takes you back to your form; for an expense, once its category has an item. Sell, Move stock and Record an expense keep what you typed. A loan application, collateral and an existing loan do not, so add the member or product first, then fill in the form.
4. If the line says **Ask the owner to add a unit first.**, you may not add it yourself. Ask the owner or an administrator to add it, then open the form again.

> **Counted in** ends its list with + Add a unit even when it has units, so you can add the one you need while you add an item.
