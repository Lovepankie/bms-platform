A loan starts as an application for a member. You send it for appraisal when it is complete.

1. Open **Loans** under Lending in the menu and tap **New loan application**.
   ![Tap New loan application](loan-application-1.webp)
2. Find the member, choose the loan product, and type how much and for how long. The product's limits show under it.
   ![Choose the member and the product](loan-application-2.webp)
3. Add a guarantor and tick the collateral if the product needs them. Then tap **Send for appraisal**.
   ![Tap Send for appraisal](loan-application-3.webp)

> If something stops it, the application stays saved as a draft and the page says what to fix.
