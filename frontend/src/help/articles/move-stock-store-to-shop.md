Stock leaves from the branch you are working in. So first switch to the store, then move the goods to the shop.

1. Open the menu and use **Switch branch** at the top to choose the store the goods leave from.
   ![Choose the store with Switch branch](move-stock-store-to-shop-1.webp)
2. Open **Move stock** under Stock. Check that From shows the store, then pick the shop in **To branch**.
   ![Pick the shop in To branch](move-stock-store-to-shop-2.webp)
3. Type part of each item's name or code and tap it. Type how many you are moving. For an item kept in cartons and pieces, type the full cartons and the pieces, or all of it in pieces: BMS shows the total in pieces before you move it. You cannot move more than the store holds.
   ![Add the items and how many](move-stock-store-to-shop-3.webp)
4. Tap **Move stock**. The stock leaves the store and is in the shop at once.
   ![Tap Move stock](move-stock-store-to-shop-4.webp)

> If your business receives moves in two steps, the button says **Send stock**. The goods are on the way until the shop receives them.
