A new shop has no items yet, so Sell finds nothing. Get it ready in three steps: your items, then your stock, then sell.

1. On **Home** or **Sell**, tap **Add your first items**, then **Add an item**. Choose the **Category**, or tap + Add a category at the foot of its list and make it right there. Type the **Description**, what it is **Counted in** and the **Selling price**, then tap **Add item**. The app makes the item code.
2. Have a long list of items? Tap **Import a list** instead, paste the rows or choose the file, and tap **Check the file**. Nothing is added until you check it.
3. Put in the stock you have. Goods you bought go on **Buy stock**. Goods already on your shelf go on **Count stock**.
4. Open **Sell**, find the item, tap it and tap **Record sale**.

> Searched on Sell and found nothing? Tap **Not in the list? Add it here**. The description holds what you typed, and after **Save the item** the item goes on the sale.
