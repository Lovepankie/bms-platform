Your administrator invites you with your email or phone, and you get a link by email, SMS or WhatsApp. You open it once to choose your password.

1. Open the link from your invitation message. The page **Choose a password** opens.
2. Type your password in **New password**, and the same again in **Type it again**. It needs at least 10 characters, and not a common password or your email or phone. Tap **Save and continue**.
3. On **Protect your account**, tap **Turn on two-step sign-in** to add a code from your phone as well, or tap **Skip for now**. If your business asks for two-step sign-in, this step cannot be skipped.
4. If you turned it on, scan the code with your authenticator app and type the **6 digit code from the app**. Save your recovery codes, tick **I have saved these codes** and tap **Continue**. BMS opens.

> The link works once and stops working after 3 days. If it says it was used or replaced, ask your administrator for a new one.

> Next time, sign in with your email or phone and the password you chose.
