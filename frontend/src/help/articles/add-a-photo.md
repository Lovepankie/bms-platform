A photo helps you and your sellers pick the right item. It shows in the item list and when you pick the item in a sale.

1. While you add an item, go to **Photo** at the end of the form. Tap **Take a picture** to use the camera, or **Choose a photo** to pick one you have. It must be a PNG, JPEG or WebP picture up to 5 MB.
2. Tap **Add item**, or **Save the item** when you add it under a search. The item is saved first, then its photo.
3. To add or change the photo later, tap **Edit** on the item. **Add a photo** or **Change the photo** is at the top, under the name.

> If the photo could not be sent, the item is still saved. Add the photo from **Edit**.
