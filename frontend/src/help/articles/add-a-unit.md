A unit is what an item is counted in, such as Piece, Kg or Metre. Stock is always counted in the item's unit. Your shop starts with Piece, Pair, Set, Box, Pack, Kg, Litre and Metre.

1. Open **Items and categories**, then **Units**, to see the units your shop has.
2. Tap **Add a unit**, type its name in **Name of the new unit**, such as Tray, and tap **Add unit**.
3. While adding an item, you can add one without leaving the item: in **Counted in**, choose + Add a unit at the foot of the list. In **Add a unit**, type the name and tap **Add unit**. The new unit is chosen for the item and everything you typed stays.
4. For things you count one by one, choose Piece under **Counted in**. Piece is the same as pc or pcs, so you do not need a unit called pc.
5. A carton is a pack of pieces, not the item's unit. Keep the item counted in Piece, then tap **Edit** on the item and, under **Cartons, boxes and other packs**, choose Carton in **Add a pack**, type 24 in **Items in one pack** and tap **Add pack**.
6. A unit such as Carton can keep a usual pack, such as 24 Piece, that an item's pack form starts from. To change it, tap **Usual pack** on the unit in Units, choose what **It holds**, change the number and tap **Save**, or tap **Remove usual pack**. Items keep the packs they have.

> A unit is never deleted. Tap **Switch off** on one you stop using; items that use it keep it.
