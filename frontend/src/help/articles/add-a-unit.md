Units come in two kinds. A **Single unit** is the one thing a customer can buy on its own, such as Piece, Metre or Litre. A **Package** holds single units, such as a Box of 10 pieces. Stock is always kept in the item's single unit. Your shop starts with the usual ones: the single units Piece, Pair, Set, Metre, Kg, Gram, Litre and Ml, and the packages Dozen, Box, Carton, Packet, Bag, Roll, Bale, Crate and Bundle.

1. Open **Items and categories**, then **Units**. The page has two parts, **Single units** and **Packages**.
2. To add your own single unit, tap **Add a single unit**, type its name, such as Tray, and tap **Add a single unit**.
3. To add your own package, tap **Add a package**, type its name, type **How many does it usually hold?** and choose the single unit, then tap **Add a package**. Each item can still hold a different number. A dozen always holds 12: the app fills in 12 and it cannot be changed. For another number, use a box.
4. While adding an item, you can add one without leaving the item: in **Single unit**, choose + Add your own at the foot of the list, type the name and tap **Add unit**. The new unit is chosen for the item and everything you typed stays.
5. For things you sell one by one, choose Piece as the **Single unit**. Piece is the same as pc or pcs, so you do not need a unit called pc.
6. A carton is a package of pieces, never the item's single unit. Keep the single unit Piece, then add the carton under **Cartons, boxes and other packs** on the item and type how many pieces one holds.
7. A package can keep a usual number, such as 24 pieces, that an item's package starts from. To change it, tap **Usually holds** on the package in Units, change the number and tap **Save**, or tap **Remove the usual number**. Items keep the packages they have.

> Tap **Delete** on a unit you stop using. It is hidden, but items that use it keep it, and **Bring back** after Show deleted switches it on again.
