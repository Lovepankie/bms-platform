An administrator adds a branch and says what it is: a shop, a store or a head office. The app makes its code from the name.

1. Open **Business set-up** from the menu, then **Branches**.
   ![Open Branches](add-a-branch-1.webp)
2. Tap **Add a branch**. Type the name and pick what it is: **Shop** sells, **Store** keeps stock and does not sell, **Head office** keeps no goods.
   ![Type the name and pick Shop or Store](add-a-branch-2.webp)
3. Tap **Add the branch**. It is in every branch list at once, and a store never sells from the moment it is added.
   ![The new branch in the list](add-a-branch-3.webp)

> If your plan has no room for another branch, the form says so. Change the plan in **Plan and billing**, or close a branch you no longer use.
