A business starts by applying. We check every application, then email you a link to choose your password and sign in.

1. On the BMS home page, tap **Apply to start**. The form **Start using BMS** opens.
2. Type the **Business name**, **Your name**, **Phone number** and **Email**, and choose your **Country**.
3. Under **What do you want to use?**, tick **Shop: sales, stock and buying stock**, **Lending: members, loans and savings**, or both. Under **How do you want to start?**, choose **Try one month free** or **Subscribe now**, and under **Pay**, choose **Monthly** or **Yearly**.
4. If a business told you about BMS, type its code in **Referred by (optional)**. Then tap **Send application**.
5. We email you a link. Open it and tap **Confirm my email**. Then we look at your application.
6. When your account is ready, we email you a link to set your password. Open it, choose your password and sign in.

> No email after a few minutes? Look in your spam folder, or apply again with the same email to get a new link.

> If we need more information, the page of your application shows **Our question**. Type **Your answer** and tap **Send answer**, and we look again.
