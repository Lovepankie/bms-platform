Import items puts a whole list of items, their prices and their stock on one screen. One tap saves them all.

1. Open **Items** and tap **Add many items**. You can also open it from **Quick add** ("Adding many? Add them as a list") or from **Import a list** on Home while your shop has no items.
2. Check **Stock goes to** at the top: the shop or store where the items are now.
3. Type one item on each row: the **Name**, the **Group**, the unit, **How many now**, the **Buying price** if you see it, and the **Selling price**. Tab or Enter takes you to the next box. Tap **Add another row** for more.
4. Is the list in Excel, Google Sheets or WhatsApp? Tap **Paste a list**, paste the lines and tap **Fill the rows**. Put the columns in this order: name, group, unit, how many, buying price, selling price. Then check each row.
5. Tap **Save the list**. BMS checks every row, saves the good ones at once and says how many it added, such as "18 items added, 2 need a look." The rows that need a look stay on the screen with what to fix under them.

> A name already in your items? Tap **Add to its stock instead**: the number you typed is added to that item's stock.

> Use a point for decimals, like 2.5. A comma only goes between thousands, like 12,500. BMS never changes a number you typed.

> No internet? Save the list anyway. It is kept on this phone and sent once when the phone is back online.

> A CSV file with codes, barcodes or packages? Open **From a file** under the rows.
