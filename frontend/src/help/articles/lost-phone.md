A lost or stolen phone that was signed in to BMS can be signed out at once, from any other phone or computer.

1. Sign in to BMS on another phone or a computer.
2. Tap your menu at the top right, then **Sign out everywhere**.
3. Tap **Sign out everywhere** again to say yes. Every phone and computer you were signed in on is signed out, this one too. The lost phone is refused the next time it opens BMS.
4. Sign in again here with your password.

> No other phone or computer at hand? Ask the owner or an administrator. On the Staff page they tap **Sign out their devices** on your card, then **Yes, sign them out**. You stay on your business's staff and sign in again with your password.

> Think someone saw your password too? Choose a new one with **Forgot password?** on the page where you sign in. A new password also signs you out everywhere.

> BMS keeps you signed in while you open it at least once every 30 days, and asks for your password again after 90 days. Lock your phone's screen, so a lost phone does not open BMS for someone else.
