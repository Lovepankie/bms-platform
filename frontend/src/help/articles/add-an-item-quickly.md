Quick add puts a new item, its prices and how many you have in one form. One tap saves them all.

1. Open **Quick add** under Stock in the menu. On a phone it is under **More**. A shop with no items yet also shows **Quick add an item** on Home and Sell.
2. Type the **Name** and the **Selling price**. The **Buying price** is optional. Type **How many do you have here now?** and check **Where**: it starts with the branch you are in, and you can pick a store.
3. Leave the **Category** to put the item under General, or pick or add one. The unit starts as a piece. Add a **Photo** if you like.
4. Tap **Add**. BMS saves the item and its stock together and says what it added, such as "Test bulb added: 600 pc at Test Store". Tap **Add another** for the next item, or **Sell it** to put it on a sale.

> Already have an item with that name? Tap **Use the existing one and just add stock**. The number you typed is added to the stock it already has there: 50 and 10 more make 60. Other items are never changed.

> Quick add needs the internet. Without it, connect, then tap **Add** again.
