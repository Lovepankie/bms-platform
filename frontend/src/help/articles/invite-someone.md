You invite a person with their phone number. BMS gives you a link to send them; they open it once to choose their password.

1. Open **Staff** under Business in the menu, then tap **Invite someone**.
   ![Tap Invite someone](invite-someone-1.webp)
2. Type the full name and the phone number. Pick the role: it decides what the person can see and do. Then tick where they work.
   ![Type the name, phone and role](invite-someone-2.webp)
3. Tap **Invite**. Copy the link, or tap **Send on WhatsApp**. The link is shown only now.
   ![Send the link on WhatsApp](invite-someone-3.webp)

> The link works once and stops working after 3 days. If it is lost, make a new one from the Staff page.
