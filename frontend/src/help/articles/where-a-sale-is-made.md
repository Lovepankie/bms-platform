If the owner of your business wants it, the app notes where your phone is each time you record a sale or something used or damaged. It never stops a sale.

1. The first time you open **Sell** or **Usage and damage**, the app says why it asks: the owner wants to see where each sale is made, so a sale can be checked later. Only the owner and the business's administrators see it. Tap **Allow**, then allow it on your phone too, or tap **No thanks**. It asks only once on each phone.
2. When you record a sale or a used or damaged item, the app sends where your phone was with it, if your phone knows. With no location, or if you said no, the sale is recorded just the same.

> Only the owner and the business's administrators see it, and only as a distance from the shop, for example "40 m from the shop". Other staff never see it, and you do not see it either. Nobody sees your phone's position on a map.

> Nothing is noted between sales, and nothing when the app is closed. The app notes only the moment you record a sale or a used or damaged item.

> A phone indoors often gives a rough position, so a few hundred metres from the shop does not by itself mean the sale was made somewhere else. The owner sees how sure the phone was.

> If the business closes and its data is removed, these notes are removed too. Changed your mind? Allow or block location for BMS in your phone's settings.
